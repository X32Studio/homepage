A working essay on the deep structure of agent technology. It argues the scattered techniques — prompting, RAG, ReAct, context engineering, MCP, skills, harnesses, loops — are not a pile of tricks but one repeated motion: **the model hits the edge of what it has already compressed, external structure grows around it, and that structure later gets partially absorbed back into the model.**

One-line thesis: **the agent is the model outside the model.** Published as PDF in English and 中文.
