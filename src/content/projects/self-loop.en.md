**self-loop** is Layer 1 of the X32 Agentic Stack: in the AI era, human attention is the only real bottleneck, and how you govern your agent swarm defines your leverage.

Without governance, coding agents devolve into a chaotic mob that grades its own homework and burns you out. `self-loop` organizes **Frame Reviewer**, **Critic**, **Actor**, and cross-vendor **Judge** behind hard Git/CI gates — turning chaos into a trustworthy agent organization so you can sit at a street-side café drinking coffee while verified software ships.

**Chaos in. Trustworthy agent organization and verified software out.**
