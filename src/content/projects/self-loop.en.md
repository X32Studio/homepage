**self-loop** is Layer 1 of the X32 Agentic Stack: an autonomous software delivery engine that turns a written purpose and hard engineering constraints into verified commits, tests, and audit records.

Instead of letting one agent grade its own homework, `self-loop` isolates **Critic** (framing & attack cases), **Actor** (implementation in owned directories), and **Judge** (independent verification against held-out checks) across clean worktrees and append-only round records.

**Purpose in. Verified production software out.**
