Most AI developer portfolios look like a shelf of disconnected weekend demos. X32 Studio is built the opposite way: every open-source repository is one load-bearing layer of a single closed-loop system designed to multiply what one engineer can ship from strategy to delivery.

## Why four layers?

When you operate as a one-person company (OPC) and directly responsible individual (DRI), bottlenecks never appear in just one place. Writing code faster does not help if your agent skills collide, your research resets every session, or your architecture lacks first-principles grounding.

The **X32 Agentic Stack** closes all four loops together:

1. **L1 · `self-loop` (`v0.4.10`) — Autonomous Delivery Engine**: Separates **Critic**, **Actor**, and **Judge** across isolated roles and fresh worktrees. Instead of hoping a single chat session stays disciplined, `self-loop` turns written purpose and hard constraints into verified commits, passing test suites, and append-only decision records.
2. **L2 · `System of Skills` — Skill Architecture & Composition**: Single-skill tutorials break down once you run ten or twenty capabilities in production. This guide formalizes disjoint trigger envelopes, single-owner capabilities, and clean handoff contracts.
3. **L3 · `Argus` — Persistent Deep-Research Instrument**: Packaged as a Claude Code skill with a live visual dashboard, Argus grows a persistent knowledge tree across rounds so long-range technical and market inquiries compound rather than evaporate.
4. **L4 · `Agent DAO` — First-Principles Monograph**: Anchors the whole stack on one structural thesis: **the agent is the model outside the model.** When models hit their compression boundary, external structure grows around them — harnesses, skills, memory trees, and verification loops — before partially absorbing back in.

## How the loop closes

Theory without execution turns into essays nobody builds on; execution without theory turns into brittle prompt glue. In X32 Studio, **Argus** gathers deep field signal, **Agent DAO** distills durable principles, **System of Skills** structures reusable capabilities without trigger drift, and **`self-loop`** ships verified code continuously.
